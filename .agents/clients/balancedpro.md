# BalancedPro

## Your role
Owner. This is your company.

## What they do
Multi-vendor telecom provider. Sources and manages telecom services — internet, voice, cloud communications, SD-WAN — from multiple carriers and vendors, then delivers the right combination to each business customer. One relationship, multiple vendors, better outcomes.

## Who the customer is
Any business that buys or manages telecom services. The buyer is typically an IT director, operations lead, or business owner who is overpaying, underserved, or drowning in fragmented vendor relationships.

## Active campaign
**Spectrum** — currently in build. Context: selling Spectrum Business services to B2B customers through the BalancedPro channel.

## Voice
Direct and warm. Confident, clear, and human. Never cold, never salesy. Talk like a knowledgeable partner who is on the customer's side.

### In practice
- "We'll handle it" not "our team will facilitate the coordination"
- "You're overpaying" not "there may be cost optimization opportunities"
- "Here's what we recommend and why" not "here are some options to consider"

## Avoid
- Telecom jargon without plain-language translation
- Vague benefit claims ("best in class," "cutting edge")
- Anything that sounds like a vendor pitch rather than a partner conversation

## Blast radius
Customers, published content, pricing. Anything going out to customers or live on the site requires your approval before it lands.
